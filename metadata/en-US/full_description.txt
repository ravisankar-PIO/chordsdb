Isai is a lyrics and guitar chords viewer designed for live worship performance. It fetches your song library from a user-configured URL, caches it locally for offline use, and displays lyrics with guitar chords in a clean, readable format.

<b>Features</b>
• Auto-scroll — tap Play to begin smooth continuous scrolling at your chosen speed. Adjust speed on the fly with the + and − buttons, pause to hold your place, or stop to return to the top.
• Transpose — raise or lower all chords on the current song by any number of semitones using the ▲ ▼ buttons shown above the lyrics.
• Capo indicator — when a song specifies a capo fret, it is shown clearly above the lyrics.
• Offline cache — songs are stored locally and refreshed in the background on a configurable schedule (default: every few hours).
• Search — quickly filter songs by title, lyrics, composer, or any other field.
• QR code import — scan a QR code to configure the songs URL without typing.

<b>Setup</b>
You need a URL pointing to a songs XML file. Enter it in Settings, or scan a QR code. The app will fetch and cache your library automatically.

Translation and chord display can each be toggled in Settings.
